Export Process Guide

  1. Share your requirements. Tell us the product, quantity, destination, packaging preference, company, and business email.
  2. Review availability. We discuss the available origin, process, grade, lot details, and buyer specifications.
  3. Receive a quotation. Commercial details and shipment requirements are clarified directly with the buyer.
  4. Confirm packing. Standard packing is 60 kg jute bags. GrainPro or hermetic liners can be requested.
  5. Coordinate documentation. Export documentation requirements are reviewed as part of the shipment process.
  6. Coordinate shipment. Communication continues through preparation and shipment coordination.

Commercial terms: minimum order quantity 500 kg; standard packing is 60 kg jute bags; GrainPro or hermetic liners are available on buyer request.
